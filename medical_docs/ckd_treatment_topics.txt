Title: CKD Treatment Topics for Patient Education
Source: Educational demo document.

Possible discussion topics include blood-pressure management, assessment of albuminuria, diabetes-related kidney protection when appropriate, individualized nutrition advice, management of anemia and mineral-bone issues, and monitoring of eGFR, urine albumin, electrolytes, symptoms, and cardiovascular risk.
